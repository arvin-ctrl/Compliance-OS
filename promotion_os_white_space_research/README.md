# Promotion / Regulatory Commerce White-Space Research Project

## Mission

Determine whether a sufficiently large, defensible enterprise software opportunity exists at the intersection of:

- promotion / sweepstakes administration
- incentive decisioning
- regulatory action authorization
- jurisdiction-aware policy management
- compliance evidence and auditability
- identity / geolocation / risk signals
- generic policy-as-code infrastructure

This is **not a software build project yet**.

The project must first prove that a meaningful white space exists that is large enough to justify building a company around it.

## Core question

> Is there an enterprise problem in promotional or regulated commerce that existing products solve poorly enough that large companies would buy, integrate, or switch to a new product?

The answer may be **GO**, **PIVOT**, or **KILL**.

A KILL result is acceptable and preferable to inventing a weak opportunity.

## Research organization

### Research agents
One research agent is assigned to each company.

### Category managers
Managers review the company agents in their category, reject weak claims, normalize findings, and identify category-level gaps.

### Chief synthesis manager
Combines all categories into a unified capability matrix and identifies candidate white spaces.

### Red-team manager
Attempts to disprove every candidate opportunity using competitor capabilities, build-vs-buy logic, switching costs, buyer incentives, and adjacent substitutes.

### Final decision manager
Produces the final GO / PIVOT / KILL recommendation only after the red-team pass.

## Primary deliverables

1. 15 standardized competitor reports
2. evidence ledger with citations/URLs/access dates
3. normalized feature/capability matrix
4. category summaries
5. enterprise buyer/job-to-be-done analysis
6. candidate white spaces
7. red-team rejection report
8. surviving opportunities ranked by attractiveness
9. final GO / PIVOT / KILL memo
