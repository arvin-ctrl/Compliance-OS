## Required adjacent-company expansion

Each research agent must identify at least **two additional substitutes or competitors**
encountered during research.

Category managers must deduplicate these discoveries and add any material omitted
competitor to an appendix.

The chief synthesis manager must determine whether the initial 15-company set is
broad enough. If a major product category is missing, spawn additional research agents.

## Categories

### Manager A — Promotion Administration
Focus: sweepstakes, contests, instant win, official rules, drawing, fulfillment,
AMOE, legal administration, promotion APIs.

### Manager B — Incentive & Loyalty Decision Engines
Focus: real-time event evaluation, rules engines, promotion conditions, customer
state, campaigns, budgets, effects, decision latency, enterprise APIs.

### Manager C — Compliance Signal & Risk Platforms
Focus: geolocation, identity, KYC/KYB, fraud, case management, regulatory workflow,
decisioning, compliance orchestration, auditability.

### Manager D — Policy Infrastructure
Focus: policy-as-code, authorization, versioning, policy distribution, control
planes, policy testing, logs, explainability, multi-tenant governance.
