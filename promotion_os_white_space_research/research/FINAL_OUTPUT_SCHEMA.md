# Final Opportunity Output Schema

Every surviving opportunity must use this format.

## Opportunity name

### Buyer
Exact team/title likely to own the budget.

### Job to be done
One sentence.

### Current process / stack
Products + internal tooling + services presently used.

### Failure
What specifically remains painful?

### Consequence
Cost, risk, delay, revenue impact, engineering burden, or compliance exposure.

### Proposed wedge
Smallest product that solves the pain.

### Why current vendors do not solve it
Evidence-backed.

### Why incumbent cannot trivially add it
Or acknowledge that it can.

### Why customer would adopt another vendor
Concrete enterprise switching/add-on thesis.

### Integration model
Where product sits in architecture.

### Data / workflow moat
What improves with usage?

### Regulatory / legal boundary
What the software determines versus what counsel must approve.

### Most dangerous competitor
Company or stack.

### White-space score
0–100.

### Red-team result
SURVIVES / WEAKENED / KILLED.

### Recommendation
BUILD MVP / CUSTOMER DISCOVERY / MONITOR / REJECT.
