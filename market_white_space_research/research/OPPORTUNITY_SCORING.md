# Opportunity Scoring Framework (Wave 2+)

Score each dimension 0–5, multiply by weight, normalize to 100.

| # | Dimension | Weight | What 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pain severity & frequency | 15% | Recurring (weekly+) operational pain with emotional charge in the evidence |
| 2 | Budget proof | 15% | People ALREADY pay for this pain: headcount, services, fines, or hated software |
| 3 | Competitive gap | 12% | Current products/stacks/services demonstrably fail a defined segment |
| 4 | Forcing function | 10% | A deadline, regulation, platform requirement, or money movement compels action |
| 5 | Founder+agents feasibility | 12% | One founder + AI-agent team can ship a credible wedge in ≤90 days |
| 6 | Distribution reachability | 10% | Identifiable watering holes / communities / channels a solo founder can reach; short sales cycle |
| 7 | AI-structural advantage | 8% | Agent-heavy delivery collapses a labor cost incumbents can't match without breaking their model |
| 8 | Moat path | 8% | Workflow lock-in, proprietary data accumulation, network, or content asset grows with usage |
| 9 | Expansion ceiling | 5% | Wedge credibly expands toward a $100M+ revenue shape |
| 10 | Durability | 5% | Survives incumbent AI releases, platform shifts, and model-capability jumps |

## Hard gates (any failure = kill, regardless of score)
- **No budget proof** — nobody currently pays anything (money or salaried time) for this pain
- **Unreachable buyer** — requires enterprise procurement/sales infrastructure a solo founder lacks
- **Thin-wrapper risk** — the whole product is one model call away from being a ChatGPT/incumbent feature; no workflow, data, or integration depth
- **Head-on collision** — same wedge, same segment as a well-funded, competent, currently-shipping incumbent
- **Platform hostage** — viability depends on one platform's continued tolerance (single-API businesses)
- **Regulated practice** — value requires the vendor itself to practice law/medicine/accountancy without a licensed-professional structure

## Displacement sentence (required per surviving opportunity)
> Current solution = [stack/service/headcount] costing [evidence-backed amount].
> New product = [wedge]. The customer switches because [quantified reason].

## Labels
- 80–100: pursue — candidate for top picks
- 70–79: strong — red team decides
- 60–69: park — revisit if conditions change
- <60: kill

Red-team survival is required for any top pick, in addition to score.
