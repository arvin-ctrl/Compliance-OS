## 4. The wedge

**Not a CRM. The RE7/Blackbaud wing of the H16 "Exit Factory": an agent-powered migration line + archive, sold per-organization (direct and through consultants/destination vendors).** Per org, flat-price:

1. **Extract:** full SQL backup via Blackbaud's committed Bulk Data Delivery/On-Demand path [H15-E6] (fallback: Export-module playbook where SQL is withheld); parse the documented RE schema (RECORDS, GIFT, GIFTSOFTCREDIT, tribute joins, attributes, actions/notes) [H15-E25].
2. **Triage scorecard:** what converts cleanly to the chosen destination (Bloomerang/LGL/Neon/DonorPerfect/Salesforce-NPSP), what maps with playbook workarounds, what only the archive will hold; destination-cost comparison against the NXT renewal quote.
3. **Agent migration + cleanup:** dedupe, address/salutation normalization, campaign/fund/appeal mapping, pledge-state reconstruction — the labor line LGL pegs at 60% of switch cost [H15-E20].
4. **Verification pack:** machine-diffed totals — lifetime giving by constituent, totals by fund/campaign/appeal/year, pledge balances, receipt history — old vs new, signed before cutover ("your numbers tie out or you don't pay"). Kills the "Created Date integrity" class of failure [H15-E18].
5. **Permanent read-only archive:** searchable web archive of the full RE history (constituent timelines, notes/actions, queries/report outputs, attachments) — ends the zombie-contract problem ($4–15k/yr to keep history readable) and the renewal-trap hostage dynamic [H15-E10, E12].
6. **Consultant/vendor dashboard:** white-label batch pipeline so Heller/JCA-class firms and destination-vendor onboarding teams (the six-month-backlog holders [H15-E18]) can run n clients through the factory.

**Explicitly does NOT do:** build or host a donor CRM; compete with Bloomerang/LGL/Givebutter on the destination; ongoing gift processing; fundraising advice.

**≤90 days, founder + agents? Yes, scoped.** The extraction input is a standard SQL backup [H15-E6]; the schema is publicly documented by practitioners [H15-E25]; verification is deterministic arithmetic on gift tables; archive v1 is rendered read-only history. v1 scope: RE7/NXT → Bloomerang + LGL + CSV/archive-only, with Bonterra/NFG exports (per-vendor CSV) as the fast-follow second source. Salesforce NPSP and Neon/DonorPerfect loaders in v2. Bounded by triage: files v1 can't verify get routed to partner consultants at a services price.

## 7. AI-structural advantage

The product collapses a labor line the market itself prices at "60% of the total investment" of a CRM switch [H15-E20] — schema mapping, cleanup, transformation, reconciliation — into agent runtime plus a deterministic verification harness. Every incumbent is structurally barred from following: Blackbaud will never build fidelity exits (retention is its whole motion [H15-E5]); destination vendors staff onboarding as a cost center and systematically under-deliver it [H15-E18]; consultants bill the hours the agents delete [H15-E27]; Omatic monetizes staying connected to Blackbaud [H15-E26]. The RE schema's documented complexity (soft credits, tributes, attributes, action-notes [H15-E25]) is exactly the kind of finite, learnable structure that compounds into an agent playbook — one factory run per org, thousands of orgs, same tables.

## 8. Moat path

Same accumulation engine as H16, second vertical: (1) **source-schema playbooks** (RE7 versions, NXT export shapes, NFG/EveryAction/eTapestry exports) that improve per org processed; (2) **verification corpus** — before/after diffs across hundreds of donor databases, the QA asset no consultant accumulates; (3) **archive annuity + system-of-record position** for the org's pre-migration history (audit answers, lifetime-giving proofs); (4) **channel embed** — white-label deliverables inside consultant engagement letters and vendor onboarding funnels. Honest thin-wrapper assessment: a one-shot CSV converter would be commoditized quickly (Lightfield-class generic CRM-migration agents already exist for B2B SaaS); the durable assets are the verification discipline, the RE-specific playbook depth, and the archive. Ship all three or don't ship. The standalone-CRM version of H15 has NO moat available to it — that market's moat (switching costs + community trust) is already Bloomerang's and Givebutter's.

## 9. Risks & unknowns

1. **The sunset keeps slipping** (already H1-2027 → late-summer-2027; a vendor date, grade B). *Test:* track Blackbaud community product updates quarterly; the 12-month-notice commitment [H15-E6] gives runway. *Mitigant:* the renewal ratchet forces decisions datelessly [H15-E9]; Bonterra feedstock is sunset-independent. *Kill trigger:* Blackbaud announces indefinite database-view continuation AND renewal increases moderate below ~10%.
2. **Destination vendors close the fidelity gap** (Bloomerang staffs up onboarding or buys an agent-migration capability; "migrations included" becomes actually good). *Test:* monthly sampling of conversion-timeline complaints on Capterra/G2; 5 discovery calls with onboarding leads (also our white-label pipeline). *Kill trigger:* median vendor conversion <4 weeks with soft-credit/tribute fidelity documented.
3. **WTP undershoots** — orgs expect the destination vendor to eat migration cost ("Bloomerang includes it"). *Test:* 10 paid-pilot offers at $2.5–7.5k into the two RE Facebook groups + 1 consultancy; measure close rate and clearing price. *Kill trigger:* <$1,500 clearing price direct AND no consultant/vendor channel deal by day 60.
4. **SQL-backup access friction** — Blackbaud slow-walks Bulk Data Delivery for departing customers, or self-hosted backups arrive corrupt/versioned oddly. *Test:* 3 pilot extractions end-to-end; measure request-to-delivery days and parse success. Written commitment exists [H15-E6]; contract terms may still gate timing. *Kill trigger:* median delivery >30 days or <80% parse success without manual SQL surgery.
5. **Base too enterprise-skewed for a solo motion** — RE decisions at universities/hospitals run through IT committees and RFPs (the segment Manager 1 warned about). *Test:* first-10 pipeline composition; count orgs where a development director + ED can sign a <$10k migration without procurement. *Mitigant:* the Bonterra/eTapestry cohorts are definitionally small-org. *Kill trigger:* >70% of inbound requires procurement processes.
