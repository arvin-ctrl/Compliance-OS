## Pick 1 — Payer-Defense Bundle, led by denial & underpayment defense (H01)

**The company:** the defense layer for small healthcare practices in the payer-AI era.
Payers deployed AI first (BCBSIL blanket downcoding live 2026-07-01; 2,400 claims cut at
one small psychiatric practice in one month; Illinois's ban not effective until 2028).
The defense labor is priced in public: 4–10% of collections outsourced, $37.5–50.5k
denial specialists, 20–35% contingency recovery — while ~35–60% of denials are never
resubmitted and ~70% overturn when appealed.

**The wedge (v1):** ERA-native batch downcode/underpayment defense — practices or
micro-billing companies upload 835 remittance files under BAA; the product flags
downcodes, underpayments, and expiring appeal windows in dollars; agents draft
provider-reviewed appeal packets; a recovery ledger tracks outcomes. Explicitly NOT an
appeal-letter generator (that variant scored ~65 and is crowded).

**Why it survives red team:** every armed kill-trigger checked cold on 2026-08-27 —
Muni still ships no ERA/batch/underpayment layer, Assured still enterprise-gated,
Waystar/Tebra no self-serve SKU. The window is competitive (quarters), not calendar,
and a failed quarter still permits the January Exit Factory pivot — the reverse is
not true. Same-buyer expansion: credentialing (H02, module 1, its own PURSUE 80.8
case) → prior-auth assembly (H03) → EVV home care (H09, when its sub-thesis clears).

**First 10 customers:** recruit from the live BCBSIL/BCBSTX downcode threads
(r/CodingandBilling, 19.3k members; r/therapists), Illinois behavioral-health groups,
and 2–3 micro-billing companies (HBMA directory) who each bring multiple practices.

**90-day plan:**
- Weeks 1–3 — "ERA X-ray" concierge pilots: 5 practices/billing firms upload 90 days
  of 835s; deliver dollar-quantified findings in days; meter every human minute.
- Weeks 3–8 — convert at $299–599/mo (or 10–15% of a batch recovery campaign);
  ship the packet-not-portal appeal flow (human submits; no portal-bot ToS exposure).
- Weeks 8–13 — prove the number that matters: recovered dollars per practice ≥10x
  subscription; publish the first case study into the same threads.

**Kill criteria (pre-registered):** <3 of 5 pilots convert at those terms; flagged
recoverable dollars <10x subscription; human-submit flow saves <60% of minutes-per-denial;
or a competitor raises ≥$10M AND ships ERA-level batch defense for this tier before
customer #25. Standing watch: Muni/Aegis funding, Availity policy, payer countermeasures.

## Pick 2 — Exit Factory, led by the QuickBooks Desktop wind-down (H16)

**The company:** agent-powered, flat-price-per-file migration + cleanup + signed
verification diff + permanent read-only archive, sold through accounting firms holding
multi-client Desktop books. Displaces a published labor line: 20–40 billable hours
(≈$2,000–8,000) per file, against Intuit's own documented data-loss list and verified
price waves (Oct 1, 2026 next — five weeks out). Generalizes to the sunset conveyor:
Blackbaud RE7 (H15-B, validated 77), cleanup backlogs (H17), then the watchlist
(Sage 50, PCLaw archives, NRx).

**Red-team conditions (binding):**
1. **Feasibility spike FIRST:** payroll-YTD fidelity under QBD SDK access restrictions
   is the slate's hardest technical risk — prove the $0.01-tie-out claim on real files
   before selling anything.
2. Pitch firms as **capacity expansion**, not cost removal — migration hours are firm
   revenue; the factory lets a firm take 3x the files this season.
3. The archive-annuity and two-channel conveyor economics are unproven — treat as
   upside, not plan.

**First 10 customers:** 3 accounting/bookkeeping firms with 25+ Desktop files
(r/QuickBooks, r/Bookkeeping, TaxProTalk, ProAdvisor communities — where the Oct-1
renewal rage is live), direct-to-SMB secondary.

**90-day plan:** weeks 1–2 the payroll-fidelity spike on 3 donor files; weeks 2–6
$499/file money-back pilots ("trial balance ties or free"); weeks 6–13 scale to 25 files
across 3 firms, standing up the verification-diff pack and archive as the differentiator.

**Kill criteria:** fidelity spike fails on payroll-heavy files; clearing price <$150/file;
<3 firms sign in 45 days; Intuit ships a fidelity-grade free migration tool or another
Desktop extension. **Role in portfolio:** the designated January-2027 pivot if Pick 1's
first quarter fails — its calendar window (2026–2028 cohort) survives a one-quarter delay.
