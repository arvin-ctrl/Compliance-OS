# Executive Summary — Market White-Space Discovery

Director · 2026-08-27 · Companion: `opportunity_ranking.md`, `top_picks.md`, `red_team_report.md`

## What the program did

Twelve signal scouts (plus two re-scouts) swept the 2026 market by *evidence type* —
EOLs, regulatory deadlines, job postings that are secretly software, operator complaints,
money friction, review-platform gaps — and surfaced ~97 evidenced pain candidates with
~550 sourced artifacts. Manager 1 promoted 20 hypotheses; 20 validators produced full
dossiers with fresh competitive research; Manager 2 re-verified 44 load-bearing claims
(zero fabrications) and put five targets to an independent red team, which re-checked
every armed kill-trigger on primary sources the same day. Five hypotheses were killed
outright, two parked, the rest consolidated into two company shapes and three picks.

## The finding

The 2026 white space for a founder + AI-agent team is not "AI for X." It is two
repeated market shapes:

1. **When the counterparty automates, sell the defense.** Payers and insurers deployed
   AI against small operators first — blanket downcoding, EVV claim-matching denials,
   photo-estimate short-pays. The defense labor is already priced in public (percent of
   collections, salaried specialists, per-supplement fees), and no funded player serves
   the small-operator side of the fight.
2. **Sunsets are conveyors.** Vendors keep imposing dated migrations (QuickBooks
   Desktop, Blackbaud RE7, PCLaw, QS/1), each displacing thousands of dollars of priced
   labor per file — and nobody runs the exit as a factory.

And one anti-finding that killed four hypotheses: a mandate whose compliance is given
away free (UK MTD, EU e-invoicing) creates incumbent races, not markets. The forcing
function must gate money the buyer is already losing.

## The recommendation

**Concentrate on Pick 1: the Payer-Defense Bundle, led by ERA-native denial &
underpayment defense for small practices (validated 85/100; red team: SURVIVES).**
One API-free wedge — upload remittance files, get dollars-flagged downcodes and
expiring appeal windows, agent-drafted provider-reviewed appeals, a recovery ledger.
First customers are already complaining in named threads; the expansion path
(credentialing → prior auth → home care) shares the same buyer.

Hold **Pick 2, the Exit Factory (81/100; SURVIVES as wedge)** as the calendar-protected
pivot: run its payroll-fidelity spike now, sell only if the spike passes, and step in
fully in January if Pick 1's quarter fails. Spend two founder-weeks on **Pick 3, the
club-money AR audit** in your own network — research only, cheapest test in the program,
H13 (law-firm trust accounting) as the named alternate.

## The honest caveat

No surviving target owns a structural moat on day one. Every defense is a 2–6-quarter
execution gap in a visibly warming category, and every core value claim — recovered
dollars per practice, $0.01 migration tie-outs, $150-per-repair-order deltas — is still
a pilot hypothesis. The slate is a bet that one founder with an agent fleet out-executes
funded teams inside closing windows. What makes the bet rational is that every pick
carries pre-registered kill criteria that resolve in weeks for hundreds — not hundreds
of thousands — of dollars, and a pre-planned pivot if the first bet dies.
