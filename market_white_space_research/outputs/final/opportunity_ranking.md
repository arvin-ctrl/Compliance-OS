## The two program-level findings

1. **"When the counterparty automates, sell the defense."** The strongest cluster
   (H01/H09/H20) is one repeated market shape: a payer/insurer deployed AI against
   small operators (downcoding, EVV claim-matching, photo estimates), the defense
   labor is priced in public, and no funded player serves the small-operator side.
2. **"Sunsets are conveyors, not events."** The second-strongest shape (H16/H15-B/H17,
   plus watchlist NRx, Sage 50, PCLaw archives): vendor-imposed migrations with dated
   deadlines, displacement priced at 20–40 billable hours/file, and a factory that
   generalizes across sunsets.

Both findings echo round 1's core lesson (forcing functions win), refined: mandates
where compliance is given away for free (H04, H06) create no market — the forcing
function must gate MONEY the buyer already loses.
