# SHARED RESEARCH BRIEF — Construction Revenue Recovery

## THESIS UNDER TEST
Is there a large, defensible opportunity in helping contractors recover or protect revenue lost because
claims, change-order entitlements, notice deadlines, delay evidence, contract clauses, and project records
are fragmented across documents and systems?

Do NOT assume the product is a "claims copilot." The true wedge may be narrower, broader, or nonexistent.

## CORE HYPOTHESIS (the pipeline being evaluated)
Contract + RFIs + daily reports + emails + schedules + meeting minutes + photos
-> commercial event detection
-> entitlement / notice matching
-> evidence collection
-> recoverable-value estimate
-> notice / claim package

## FOUNDER CONSTRAINTS (apply to every judgement)
- Founder is initially SOLO. Penalize anything needing a large team, many deep integrations,
  multi-year procurement, huge proprietary datasets, or 24/7 staffing before first revenue.
- Prefer V1s startable with file upload, email forward, CSV, or a lightweight API.
- A MISSING FEATURE IS NOT WHITE SPACE. Absence must be paired with proven, paid-for pain.

## RESEARCH STANDARDS (non-negotiable)
- Use current web research. Prioritize, in order: official product pages, developer/API docs, pricing pages,
  published case studies, marketplace/app-store listings, SEC/investor material, court/industry reports,
  then G2/Capterra/Reddit reviews, then press.
- CITE A URL for every material claim. Include the source's date where visible.
- Label anything unconfirmed as `UNVERIFIED`. NEVER invent numbers, pricing, funding, or customer counts.
  A clearly-labelled "could not verify" is worth more than a plausible guess.
- Prefer 2024-2026 sources. Flag when your best source is older than 2023.
- Quote reviewers verbatim where you use them.
