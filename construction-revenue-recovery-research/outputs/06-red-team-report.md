## The three findings that changed the ranking

**1. The $400/hr rate was wrong.** The program's own consultant report derives Diales — the only listed
pure-play — at **£165–200/hr**. The displaced block is worth $24–108K, not $80–240K, making $7,500/matter
**15–70% of value created, not 3–9%**.
*Final-decision adjudication: the red team over-corrected. Diales is UK/ME/APAC with an assumed denominator;
the correct US analyst basis is $175–275/hr, giving $3,000–5,000/matter. **The price was never the problem.***

**2. "Bid the sub-$5m matters you turn away" is arithmetically false.** On the corpus's own table the tool
removes **13–22% of matter hours**. A matter uneconomic at $240K remains uneconomic at $190K. This was the
*sole* rebuttal to channel cannibalisation, and it does not survive its own numbers.

**3. Discovery risk lands opposite to expectation.** Per-matter, counsel-engaged work fits *William A. Gross*
(Hill Int'l) — **held protected**. Subscription or contractually-required output fits *Alta Refrigeration* and
*G.M. Harston* — **held not protected**. Plus FRCP 26(a)(2)(B)(ii): "considered" is read as anything the
expert received or read. **This wounds Day 20, not Matter Zero — the opposite of the promotion the red team
itself recommended.**

## One failed kill, which strengthened the survivor

Public Contract Code **§9204 expressly exempts Transportation**, so the Caltrans PCR regime is not
statutorily displaced. The attack was attempted and failed.

## Required reshaping of survivors

  purgeable**; job-cost it per project; **buy a PSP/CCP/PE to sign every TIA**; California only;
  **do not build until the CPRA request returns**.
- **Matter Zero** — stop selling chronology; sell the **entitlement layer on top of** aiR/Everlaw at
  $2,500–4,000/matter; delete the sub-$5m claim; solve privilege and outside-counsel-guidelines before
  pitching.
- **Write-Off Autopsy** — free, anonymised, counsel-mediated, verbal readout only.

## The single most likely reason the whole thesis fails

> **Assembled evidence monetises as *savings for payers* and *hours for professionals* — never as *recovery
> for claimants*. Gather proved this over eight years under ideal conditions, and shrank while doing it:
> eleven case studies, zero recovered compensation events in pounds, headline retreating to "Detect 40%
> More", staff down from 14 to 10.**

The final-decision manager put the probability that this generalises at **~50%**. That is the honest number,
and it is why the verdict is PIVOT gated on cheap tests rather than GO.
