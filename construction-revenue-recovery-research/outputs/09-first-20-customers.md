# 9. First-20-Customer Acquisition Plan

## The constraint that shapes everything

**Inbound is falsified five independent ways.**

1. No reviewer anywhere complains that Procore fails to protect their entitlement — the pain is **not
   articulated as a software gap**, so nobody searches for it.
2. **G2 and Capterra have no "construction claims" category at all.**
3. **Zero** of ~$250M in 2025–26 contech funding went to a claims/entitlement/notice startup.
4. In German, French, Norwegian and Dutch the pain is articulated as *legal complexity* or *behavioural
   discipline* — never as software.
5. r/ConstructionManagers is actively hostile to this idea **and heavily astroturfed** by AI-written vendor
   accounts.

**And the Procore marketplace is separately falsified:** Document Crunch has **187 installs against 17,850
Procore customers (~1.0%)** after four years of partnership. Neither Procore's 455–539 apps nor Autodesk's
194 partners include a single claims vendor.

**Therefore: association channel and direct outbound only.** No SEO, no ads, no marketplace, no Reddit.

---

## Phase 0 — Evidence, not selling *(weeks 0–6, ~$50)*

**No product. No code. No pitch.**

- **File the CPRA request to Caltrans** on ePCR filings — PCRs filed vs. supplemented-with-costed-estimate-
  and-TIA vs. waived. Free, 3–6 weeks, and it produces **a frequency number nobody in the industry has**.
- **Run the Write-Off Autopsy free** on 30–50 anonymised closed matters — counsel-mediated, verbal readout
  only. This builds the record-quality-vs-outcome dataset that does not exist anywhere.
- **Ten project-executive calls** on one question: *do you already comply with the 20-day Supplemental on
  time?* If ≥7 say yes, the wedge is dead and you have spent nothing.

The dataset is the asset. It is publishable, it is an industry first, and it is the credential a founder
without a PSP does not otherwise have.

## Phase 1 — Customers 1–6 *(weeks 4–16)*

**Target: claims consultancies of 10–50 people, and holders of state-DOT on-call claims contracts.**
Named: Trauner (~36 staff), Imperium (~23), Long International, VERTEX, CCI, RLB — plus Caltrans, FDOT and
VDOT on-call primes.

**Why them first:** the pain is an **already-invoiced line item**, the buyer is a partner who signs the same
week, and the cost is **rebilled as a disbursement** — so it never touches an IT budget or a procurement.

**Event: Construction SuperConference, 1–3 Dec 2026, Huntington Beach.** Attend with ~40 pre-booked meetings.
This is where construction counsel and claims professionals actually gather.

**Carry the corrected economics into every conversation.** The channel-conflict objection is real: the tool
removes only 13–22% of matter hours, so "you'll win the small matters you turn away" is arithmetically false
and will be caught. Sell the *entitlement layer above aiR/Everlaw* — notice register, EOT/compensable split,
Division 00 awareness — which is additive to their billing, not subtractive. **Screen out majority-T&M firms
early: for them, removing hours is revenue-negative.**

## Phase 2 — Customers 7–14 *(weeks 10–30)*

**Target: California heavy-civil DOT contractors, $50–300M revenue.**

**Channel: associations, not search.**
- **UCON** — United Contractors, 800+ member firms, ~$1,000 associate access. The single highest-leverage
  membership in the plan.
- **IRTBA** (300+), **GHCA** (~250) for later geographic expansion.
- **AGC/CFMA Construction Financial Management Conference, 28–30 Oct 2026, Las Vegas** — for the CFO, who was
  conspicuously **absent from every hostile practitioner thread**.
- **CFMA associate membership** ~$600/yr.

**The offer that opens the door: a free "PCR Readiness Check"** — distributed *by the association*, not found
by search. Take a contractor's last three PCRs and show whether a compliant costed estimate and TIA existed
within 20 days.

## Phase 3 — Customers 15–20 *(weeks 20–44)*

**Target: specialty trade contractors**, once the productivity-factor method is implemented with the
association document cited on the face of the output — which converts the AACE "black box" objection into an
audited method.

**Events:** NECA 4–7 Oct 2026 · MCAA 7–11 Mar 2027, San Diego · CFMA Annual 22–26 May 2027, Nashville.

*Caveat carried from the red team:* the MCAA-factor approach was killed as a standalone wedge because
**Ankura publishes the attack** on those factors. Use them as *one input with its derivation shown*, never as
the argument.

## Messaging

### The three that survive

**1. To the consultancy partner** — *measured on utilisation (57–73%) and margin (Diales: 3.3%)*
> "You bill 200–600 hours of chronology per matter and write much of it off against the fixed fee. Send the
> dump; get the chronology and the missing-evidence schedule back same-day."

**2. To the heavy-civil project executive** — *measured on claims filed compliantly and job margin*
> "Caltrans gives you 5 business days for the Initial PCR and 15 more for a Supplemental with an itemised
> cost estimate and a TIA. Miss it and §5-1.43A waives the claim and bars arbitration. We produce both from
> the daily reports you already file."

This is the strongest sentence in the program because **the counterparty wrote the specification.** It is the
only answer that survives *"This is like GC 101."*

**3. To the CFO** — *measured on DSO (55.2 days) and underbillings/equity (8.1%)*
> "Your unapproved change orders are unbilled WIP. Tell me what you wrote off last year — I'll show you which
> had the evidence to bill, from your own closed files, before you buy anything."

### The three that fail

| Message | Why it dies |
|---|---|
| *"Never miss a notice deadline"* | Aconex solved it at a full 3; Caltrans ePCR already emails reminders; **Levelset proved the filing was the revenue, not the alert**; Copilot answers it at $30/user/mo |
| *"AI-powered documentation — capture everything"* | **This is eSUB verbatim.** 18 years, right buyer, right message, ~60 staff. Documentation is a cost, not a benefit |
| *"AI detects entitlement automatically"* | Procore shipped detection 23 Jul 2026, and **AACE §1.2(f) blocks attribution** — the claim invites the "voodoo analysis" attack |
